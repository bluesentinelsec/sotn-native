# Selected playable Echo of Bat

While flying as Bat, A or Xbox Y requests Echo when Echo of Bat is found and
enabled. The same button still cancels menus; closing a menu cannot cast a wave
until the button is released and pressed again. Ordinary starts grant no relics.
Echo and Force of Echo can now be switched in the relic panel. Saves remain v9.

The original blueprint requests identity 52 in actual DRA slot 47. An occupied
slot drops this optional request without replacing its owner. A real child
reserves forty-nine first-free rows from the existing 1,024-row stage primitive
pool: seventeen hidden drivers and thirty-two visible strips. Exhaustion emits
the original sound and removes the child without consuming attack RNG. The pool
tags player ownership separately from stage generations; stage actors cannot
read, overwrite or release Echo rows with a coincident numeric generation.

The [Echo component](bat-echo.md) supplies original private-table wave/color
geometry and lifetime. Rendering reads copied packets from the actual reserved
rows, with original untextured Gouraud colors and quarter-add blending. Facing
and priority stay captured from birth; player position and rotation remain live.
Room entry retains its generation and rows, translates packets and advances no
extra simulation tick. Full stage initialization and Continue retire transient
owners. A wave can outlive Bat form.

Force is sampled only at birth. It initializes original spell ten from private
spell/INT/RNG/Mojo Mail data, sets the forward hitbox, and selects the least-used
original group 7..10 shared with selected Fire of Bat. Native contact applies
enemy damage/death and EXP. Enemy group cooldown survives child replacement and
Fire/Echo transitions. The strict rising-intensity threshold disables contact
before the fade; later relic toggles neither create an attack nor alter the
existing wave's limit. Echo itself spends no spell MP; Bat still pays its normal
form costs before command processing.

Public authored tests cover owned/disabled commands, normal and Force lifetime,
actual slot pressure, fragmented/full primitive snapshots, namespace collisions,
atomic failures, room retention, Fire/Echo cooldown sharing, relic switches and
keyboard/gamepad input. Private muted LLDB comparisons cover 144 supplied
factory/primitive/stat contexts, 48 successful births and 6,480 complete records.
Original factory, spell/group, wave and SDK instructions execute; allocation,
RNG, equipment, sound and destruction use explicit adapters. Original tables and
traces remain private; Debug and ASan/UBSan traces are identical. These are
controlled comparisons, not a console replay.

The ordinary development-character native sequence exercises repeated normal/
Force waves, birth-only toggles, actual row ownership, visible pixels and cleanup.
A separate contact-protected sequence defeats a placed enemy and awards EXP;
it changes no stats, position or enemy state. The adjacent-room sequence uses an
explicit room-edge start and contact protection. Isolated slot-15 save-room UI
and fresh byte-identical v9 restore preserve disabled Echo and active Force;
ordinary user saves are untouched. The ordinary, protected combat, room, Gas Cloud
cross-conversion and save sequences also pass under ASan/UBSan. The CMake-built
app passes a separate muted LLDB sequence including menu-close/held-A suppression
and normal SDL quit. These checks do not establish ordinary
acquisition, uninterrupted routes or console parity.

Full primitive sharing with selected Mist/Blink/trails and other legacy player
effects remains incomplete, as do global factory/group contention, Wolf, full
effects/audio and whole-route parity. Do not call Bat, Alucard or the game complete.
