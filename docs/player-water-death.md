original state13, animationC1 and common death phase2. This connects the existing

no pending healing/grab/script/stone/Mist, and bound real capture/upload owners.

retirement, C1 animation, voice6F6, flash1, Blink59002C and factory60031. Shared

upload. Common phase2 advances the owned packed-nibble decay and uploads its real

and selects terminal phase128. Game-over creation consumes that actual completion.

reuploads immutable source artwork over the captured decay. Repeated drawing and
scene freeze do not advance capture/decay or replay birth commands. The caller
restores image cursors, generations, texture pixels and factory reservations after
a later failed player update. Queued healing remains pending while dead and cannot
revive the player.
