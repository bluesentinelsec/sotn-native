# Selected ordinary water damage and queued contacts

Ordinary native water exposure now consumes actual retained waist depth, Holy
Symbol ownership and the gameplay clock. It connects the reconstructed
[damage component](water-damage-death.md) to real HP, shared pain flashes/sounds
and the [owned water-death sequence](player-water-death.md). It grants no relics,
changes no ordinary starting attributes and adds no fields to version-nine saves.

Stage combat queues its actual strongest contact, preserving raw original
parameters, source position/velocity and first equal-power priority. The next
player update consumes that snapshot after deferred healing and protection timers,
before commands/state control. Nonzero low-five-bit contacts take priority over
water; water replaces high-only words before protection/grab gates. Selected
existing physical, poison, curse, stone, immunity and absorption helpers now run
through this boundary. A newly born reaction receives one common animation update,
without replaying its first controller update or charging another RNG sequence.
The existing selected physical/elemental reactions remain incomplete; moving their
ownership does not establish complete original damage/equipment parity.

Unprotected kind6 exposure loses one HP on gameplay-clock multiples of ten,
independent of defense or poison. Surviving updates request original Blink4E,
refresh the shared32-update pain voice counter, and request a scrape only when HP
changes. The native shared factory owns the real eight-row effect; ordinary
optional allocation rules still apply. The final HP requests actual state13/C1
death, capture,64 committed texture decays and game over. Stone water contacts
delegate to their real stone owner. Unsupported Fury Plate still fails explicitly
because its defense-buff owner is incomplete; it is not enabled as additive armor.

Owned Holy Symbol now has its normal Relics-menu switch. Found/disabled ownership
does not protect Alucard. Menus, spell freeze and repeated draws do not consume
water HP or the voice counter. The native session retains a distinct wrapping
gameplay clock rather than using bounded saved play time. Actual stage/session
replacement retains that clock and the DRA voice counter after bootstrap without
an extra update. Neither value is serialized. Selected dead states retain timers,
button words and pending healing while their death animation continues; healing
cannot revive them. Failed player updates retain queued contact, HP, RNG and
counter state; actual texture/factory rollback remains with the native caller.

Public authored tests cover raw-contact priority, heal-before-water ordering,
protection expiry, Holy ownership, clock wrap, once-only reaction animation,
immunity/absorption/poison, stone delegation, zero-health protected stone, dead
timer/heal retention, missing/repeated clock contexts, unsupported armor, invalid
requests and late-update rollback. Relic/menu tests cover the new owned switch.
Private Debug/ASan traces match across1,440 actual player-update contexts and match
original selected water-response instructions. The comparison supplies prior
animation/resource/protection snapshots and adapts equipment/factories/sounds;
healing is supplied at the original post-heal comparison boundary. This is not a
complete player-frame, stage hit-detection, audiovisual or console replay.

Muted native Debug and ASan/UBSan checks exercise ordinary water HP with actual
Entrance volumes, private artwork, stage rows and rendering. They verify three HP
lost over30 unprotected updates, original shared flashes/scrape requests, menu
holds, real Holy enable/disable, and actual stage handoff retaining the timer/clock.
A separate explicit1HP/clock fixture verifies lethal ownership,56 particle births,
64 texture uploads and actual late-upload rollback. These checks supply position,
fixed-floor, clock, relic or queued-arrival fixtures as identified; they write no saves.

The CMake-built Debug application also runs from ordinary70HP/default relics
through complete water HP loss, C1/capture/decay and game-over creation, then exits
normally through SDL quit under muted LLDB. It supplies only an explicit water
position/fixed-floor pose, without health, clock, relic, protection or damage-result
injection. This is not an uninterrupted route or ordinary acquisition playthrough.

The original US wet-smoke birth remains explicitly unsupported; its unwritten
input is not guessed. Global fade recovery, Fury/other equipment, other water
owners, full damage/hit-detection/effect timing, shared contention, Alucard, castle
and console parity remain incomplete.
