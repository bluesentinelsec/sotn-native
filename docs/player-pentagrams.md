# Selected owned Pentagram and Bat Pentagram

Owned Pentagram73 and Bat Pentagram74 connect ordinary menu selection and either
hand input to resident weapon17, inventory/Duplicator consumption, actual shared
factory50 roots and children17..31, native global82 attacks and private textured
presentation. Ordinary starts and version-nine save fields remain unchanged.

## Ownership and timing

The hand helper counts actual item/hand children, including cold factory births,
before curse. Pending roots do not count. Chain1 and real root exhaustion prevent
use without stock or casting RNG. Successful player completion consumes once and
queues the selected grunt before the original6E4 birth sound. Last-copy consumption
retains the actual resident hand program. The generic throwing context also keeps
Neutron71 and Power of Sire72 connected to their existing owners.

Birth reserves25 actual shared primitive rows before properties, sound, flash,
shake or RNG. Optional exhaustion retires the child without those effects. The
first row is the original GT4; the remaining24 are actual GT3 packets. Texture,
24 private UV triangles and full384-word palette spans come from the resident
weapon17 program. Bat Pentagram uses its existing19F palette. No artwork or SDK
lookup tables are compiled into public source.

The original weapon17 BSS belongs to each loaded hand program:24 sets of local
vectors and24 mutable shard records. It is shared by that hand's callbacks rather
than copied into every child or allocated from another pool. Birth overwrites the
vectors; breakup overwrites the shard records. Connected-session replacement
retains this scratch without another callback; ordinary saves do not serialize it.

Birth anchors screen128/104 while retaining fractions and assigns the first
least-used attack group3..6 with one retained-item property roll. Global82 remains
active for24 callbacks, using actual active-screen, enemy-mask, slot and group
cooldown rules. It creates no Soul Steal orbs. Flash4, shake3 and sound6E4 retain
their common owners. Normal palettes alternate on the actual gameplay clock.

The next transition clears attack, initializes128 breakup updates and consumes96
libc rolls. That callback retains the primary image and does not move or project
the triangles. Subsequent callbacks update signed fixed positions and wrapping
angles, then use original SDK XYZ matrices, GTE division, winding and AVSZ3 depth.
AVSZ3 uses341, including its truncation. Degenerate triangles retain coordinates;
backfaces use the original reordered projection/palette and depth/visibility gates.
The renderer rasterizes one triangle per GT3, without a second quad triangle.
The final motion callback destroys before projection and palette work:153 total
callbacks, including birth, and127 breakup projections.

Actual camera changes retain the screen anchor. Ordered packets receive current
shake once. Menus, repeated draws and spirit freeze hold child/scratch cursors;
the shared screen-flash tail retains its original frozen behavior. Menu equipment
replacement, room retirement and forced Wolf replacement release real rows with
no extra callback. Dead-player cleanup clears attack without restarting it. Failed
player work and later shared/native upload failures preserve their actual factory,
primitive, character and RNG boundaries. Whole-stage atomicity remains separate.

## Evidence boundaries

Public authored fixtures cover SDK/GTE bounds, triangle rasterization, callback
lifetime, signed wrap, failed projection rollback, real factory/25-row commits,
frozen child and per-hand scratch holds, screen anchoring, failed breakup rollback,
no-tick row retirement and optional row exhaustion before properties/RNG. Their
metadata, triangle models and SDK samples are authored; they are not private-art
or acquisition evidence.

Private Debug/ASan callback traces agree byte for byte across16 supplied contexts
and1,232 complete original callbacks, including all25 packets and shared vectors/
shards. The original SDK instructions execute94,696 COP2 commands and937,864 GTE
register transfers through an explicit register adapter. Allocation, property-RNG,
flash, shake, sound and destruction services are also explicit adapters. Separate
projection comparisons cover100,000 supplied SDK/GTE contexts in each build,
including36,088 backfaces and27,691 degenerates.

Three further Debug/ASan comparisons agree byte for byte with original routines:
11,520 complete hand-helper calls,3,200 stock/Duplicator consumption contexts and
6,400 retained-item property calculations. Their reservation/consumption,
equipment-count, RNG and GTE leading-zero service boundaries remain explicit.

Actual native Debug and ASan/UBSan checks cover both items and both hands through the ordinary
menu and input: last-copy program retention,25 real rows,24 active attack frames,
native enemy damage, four property rolls and384 breakup rolls, private primary/
triangle pixels, flash/shake, screen anchoring and natural153-callback retirement.
Further checks exercise seven menu updates, five repeated draws, ordinary Summon
Spirit's33 frozen updates plus release, existing v9 in-memory saved-session
construction and restored last-copy input. Actual malformed-animation failure
preserves player/factories/character/RNG without queued sounds. A later Talisman
upload failure after real row commitment and breakup restores the full runtime,
per-hand scratch, primitive pool and character/RNG; retry consumes96 rolls once.
Real menu replacement retains unrelated Talisman. Eight supplied native RNG seeds
cover four grunts before6E4 and four silent outcomes.

All225 public groups pass in Debug, Release and macOS ASan/UBSan. The final
authored invincibility-field assertion also passes in each build. Ten additional
native regression protocols repeat Power of Sire and Neutron live/audio checks
and all84 food births in both builds, with normal exits and no sanitizer errors.

Supplied stock, equipment, protection, arrival, camera, RNG, malformed-animation,
upload-failure and in-memory save-room contexts are fixtures. They do not establish
ordinary acquisition, uninterrupted routes, full shared contention, whole-stage
rollback, global libc ordering or console parity. Remaining consumables, weapons,
equipment, familiars, all six [Alucard gates](alucard-status.md), castle and original
