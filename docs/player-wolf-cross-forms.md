# Selected Wolf cross-form conversions

The native session now connects direct Wolf/Bat/Mist conversions through the
original owned relic, MP and lockout gates. Wolf can enter Bat or Mist only after
its entry substates zero/eight finish. Bat can enter Wolf after its entry finishes;
Mist can request either form while its real restoration child completes. The
existing E/Q/R and Xbox RB/LB/RT controls apply. No conversion grants a relic,
refills MP, skips restoration or resets Alucard through normal form.

Wolf-to-Mist captures the actual retained body outline and shared direct-color
image. Its eight wedges use unmirrored geometry in both facings, the original
facing-dependent texture origin, signed-word width/height and byte UV wrap.
Inverted and initial zero bounds remain valid. Mist uses the original alternate
sheet permutation and page0x118; it never substitutes the saved normal/Bat sprite.
Normal/Bat captures keep their existing packed-pixel upload and clear margins.
A repeated Wolf-to-Mist-to-Wolf chain legitimately saves bank15 again; that raw
image needs no normal/Bat ARC upload and creates no saved-image Blink23 child.

The scene captures Mist at its original position before the later Wolf body
retires. Body retirement preserves global capture bounds and the owned common
image/environment. Ordered rendering retains previous-image sampling and flushes
skipped presentations. Bat entry hides the retained bank15 player packet, removes
its stale rotation/blend and follows actual Blink20 signals before switching
banks. Conversion never manufactures factory success or visual readiness.

Public fixtures cover Wolf entry-state gates, disabled/missing resources, lockouts,
script/water rejection, retained capture metadata and initial Bat packet attributes.
Snapshot fixtures cover both facing texture origins, negative anchors, odd/inverted
bounds, coordinate wrapping and unchanged outputs on invalid data/profile input.
Private instruction comparisons exercise the original BlinkWhite snapshot routine
against forty supplied bound/facing/origin contexts, including extreme word wrap
and zero/inverted bounds. The allocation boundary uses an explicit success adapter;
this is geometry evidence, not a complete original simulation or GPU comparison.

All 151 authored tests pass in Debug, Release and macOS ASan/UBSan, alongside
34 Python checks and release packaging. The final gate/snapshot regressions also
pass in all three builds. Private muted LLDB native Step/Draw checks cover all
six directed conversions in both facings, actual nonempty Wolf image retention,
Mist page/outline, hidden Bat-entry packets, MP use, normal return and transient
owner retirement. These fixtures grant the four required relics and contact
protection, while retaining ordinary development HP/MP/stats and enemy behavior.
They write no saves. A separate check of the actual CMake-built application
performs eight consecutive conversions, including Wolf/Mist/Wolf reuse, spends
seven MP, returns normally and exits via an ordinary SDL quit event. Debug and
ASan/UBSan both pass through LLDB. The explicit relic/contact fixtures are not
ordinary acquisition, uninterrupted castle traversal or console replay evidence.

Water-depth/swimming, camera shake, normal afterimages, full factory/group/GPU
contention, complete effects/audio, ordinary acquisition and whole-route/console
parity remain incomplete. These selected conversions do not make Wolf, Alucard,
the castle or the game complete. Ordinary starts grant no relics; version-nine
saves and user slots remain unchanged. Runtime traces/images stay private.
