# Selected playable Wolf

The native session now connects the owned Wolf player, body, shared factories,
combat and scene presentation. R or Xbox RT toggles found-and-enabled Soul of
Wolf. Arrows/stick steer; X/Space/Xbox A jumps and Z/Xbox X bites. Power of Wolf
enables the original faster sprint; double-tap forward. With Skill of Wolf,
Down, Down-forward, Forward then the bite button requests Wolf Charge. Forward
follows facing. Original exact-cost spell MP and learned spell-four progress
are retained. These controls grant no relics or character resources.

Actual entry/return effects and their signals drive transformation. Pending root
or flash reservations do not create readiness; a contended entry keeps its real
generation and resumes when capacity becomes available. Preserve eight collision
passes, source slope/speed quirks, lockouts and the original unchecked missing
return-factory wait. Regular form MP and actual rush hit-feedback spending commit
once to character progress; transformed frames freeze normal recovery.

The native simulation binds current body/effect contexts before player updates,
then commits body feedback once after physics and camera follow. Refreshed player
snapshots reach enemy combat and the HUD. Private bite/rush/charge definitions
retain INT/RNG/Mojo/critical metadata and enemy cooldown ownership. Physical
damage can cancel the form; ordinary movement/retirement then resumes.

The pure controller still reports original screen-space origins. The owned
player exports captured room-space sources before camera follow and rebases them
with room transfer, without another update. The shared factory adapter accepts
those exports with zero command-camera offset. A raw controller caller instead
supplies its actual capture camera. Never add a new camera or room shift twice.

Relics now switches owned Soul, Power and Skill of Wolf alongside the other
selected abilities. The found bit survives each switch; paused gameplay, MP,
RNG, body and animation remain frozen. Disabling Soul requests the original return
on the next simulation tick, including an otherwise unbilled resource tick.
Switching never grants ownership, resets transient motion or refills MP. Ordinary
starts still grant no Wolf relics; version-nine saves remain unchanged.

Public authored tests cover keyboard/virtual Xbox trigger input, ownership bits,
current-clock feedback, real contended entry, captured origins/room rebases and
atomic failure. Private muted LLDB checks of the CMake-built app cover entry,
movement, rendering, one regular MP charge, real return and ordinary SDL quit.
Separate native Step/Draw fixtures cover ordinary player damage/cancellation and
re-entry with one zombie defeat/27 EXP; QCF charging and learned progress; exact
MP10 casting followed by automatic empty-MP return; and actual Relics navigation
with frozen simulation and Soul disabling. Explicit relic/MP fixtures are separate
evidence and write no saves. They do not establish ordinary acquisition, an
uninterrupted route, unmodified full encounters or console parity.
