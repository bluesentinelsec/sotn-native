# Bound Fairy interruption, hold and recovery

The native player now supplies a bound state17 response and a dedicated shared
Blink4A producer. Ordinary Fairy activation, saved progression, the production FFF
engine branch and actual Fairy request/cleanup/resource transactions remain
required before Life Apple rescue becomes available. This adds a gated response
to the32 states with selected native producers; Sword Brothers39 and all six
[Alucard completion gates](alucard-status.md) remain open. No ordinary starts,
grants, savev9 or primitive pools change.

A committed native Fairy sceneFFF can interrupt initialized physical, water and
common death before another capture/decay update. State17 retains actual resident
capture, pivots, rotation, color and capture serials, resets velocity/substate and
holds its pose timer. Both original entry assignments have numeric draw flag4;
the physical source's DRAW_COLORS spelling does not remove entity rotation.

Full bound dead-player callbacks accept the held gameplay clock during FFF.
Ordinary duplicate-clock updates still fail, and the native engine-frame owner
remains separate. Pending healing and the sixteen player words stay held while
the player remains classified dead, even after Fairy's separate full HP/MP write.
That write requires actual bound FFF/state17 ownership, matching HP maximum and
one resource commit per episode. Ordinary Restore/SetCharacter APIs still reject
dead players. Resource restoration itself changes no control state or capture.

The real release-only engine frame remains a caller responsibility. On the next
normal full player update, state17 chooses the existing grounded or falling helper,
captures the actual pre-collision factory origin and emits one dedicated recovery
notification. The shared runtime validates that owner and requests original44/high4A
through the actual eight-root factory range. Hurt feedback retains its separate
guards. Optional factory exhaustion omits the flash; no substitute effect is born.

Recovery clears death color modulation and active/captured death presentation so
the new normal artwork can upload. It retains the original rotation for that first
recovery frame and preserves the separate capture serial for the next death.
It also retains the original entity blend mode through ordinary movement: disabling
color modulation does not reset that word. Actual damage, stone, grab, scripted
reaction and form reset owners clear the retained blend at their corresponding
normal-reset boundaries. No palette reset is invented.

Public checks cover twelve physical/water, pre-capture/captured/partial-decay and
grounded/air contexts. They exercise failed ownership/resource commits, exact held
gameplay clocks, pending-heal/timer/dead-status holds, one-shot recovery, retained
blend/rotation and a later death generation. Private original-instruction checks
match600 complete calls:288 state17,144 physical interruptions,24 water-state13
interruptions and144 common-water interruptions. Original grounded/falling,
SetStep and SetAnim helpers execute, with only the factory boundary adapted.
Draw flags, retained rotation/blend, velocities, animation words, timer5/6,
movement flags and full factory origins are compared. These direct state and
collision fixtures are separate from whole-engine replay.

Native checks compose actual full-player, frozen-stage, HUD/audio, capture/texture
and shared-factory owners in four controlled physical/water contexts, including80
full-player callbacks on the held gameplay clock, four actual eight-row recovery
flashes and1,719 rendered flash pixels. The actual timer-driven color pulse also
preserves retained water blending. Both Debug and sanitizer protocols pass. A composed loop
is not the production engine branch. Real Fairy selection, Life Apple spending,
room/session lifecycle, all cleanup owners, whole-pass rollback, remaining
stone/grab/Axe contexts and integrated acceptance still require their native
bindings and evidence. Supplied scene/resource/clock/protection/raw-contact/HP1
fixtures establish no ordinary activation, acquisition, uninterrupted traversal,
completed familiar or console parity.
